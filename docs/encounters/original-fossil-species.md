# Surviving original fossil species: encounter rarity

Amunyte and Kinkabuto now appear only on Mt. Moon B2F, each in a 1% land encounter slot, during both day and night. Levels remain 5–8. Percentages apply to generated land encounters, not to individual steps.

Previously both were 4% on 1F and 10% on B1F; B2F had Amunyte at 25% and Kinkabuto at 14%. Removed their upper-floor slots, replacing them with Zubat. B2F common duplicate slots now use Zubat/Cleffa. Solrock (day) / Lunatone (night) occupies the 4% slot formerly used by Kinkabuto, freeing the two 1% slots for the originals.

Validated both time tables against the encounter weights and confirmed no other wild table contains either species. Main ROM build passed. No live encounter-frequency sample performed.
