# Balance findings for review

Status: the continuous campaign is still in progress. Trainer teams and levels have not been changed in response to these findings, as requested.

| Location / encounter | Observed finding | Interpretation / limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route 3, Greg | Level-18 Machop against an early party around levels 7–12; starter fainted, but Weedle won using poison. | A sharp early threat, but the battle was winnable with the earned party. |
| Route 9, Isaac / Cora / Jovan | Isaac uses level 12; Cora uses Mankey 22 and Machop 25; Jovan uses Clefairy 31, Onix 33 and Amunyte 35. | Large level spread on the route to Misty’s trial. Jovan’s first encounter references a roster named `sParty_RematchMtMoonRuinManiac`. Review whether that first-battle assignment is intended. |
| Route 5, Preston | Drowzee 30, Kadabra 32, Xatu 34 defeated the early party. | Strong opponent on an early-access route; it can be bypassed. Review sight lines and whether the danger is communicated clearly. |
| Erika, three-badge challenge | Two losses, then victory with Ninetales/Ariados, Safeguard, a Max Potion and focused attacks on Tangela. | Poor coverage and the pilot’s target selection contributed to losses. These attempts do not establish that her roster needs weakening. |
| Route 8, Johan | Four level-31 Pokémon; victory, with Ninetales and Ariados fainted. | Fits the party’s level at this point better than the early Route 9 spread. The pilot initially repeated a disabled move; that harness error was corrected and must not be counted as a game defect. |
| Sabrina, four-badge challenge | First-attempt victory using six Hyper Potions; one was used on the wrong target by the pilot. | The party had several Psychic weaknesses. Record the resource cost, but distinguish it from team balance and controller mistakes. |
| Route 12, Vera | Pelipper 31, Starmie 33, Lanturn 35. First attempt lost with a depleted travelling party. A full-PP retry with Ivysaur leading and two Hyper Potions won; Ivysaur evolved afterward. | Recovery and preparation materially changed the result. The initial loss alone is insufficient evidence for a nerf. |

Additional contrast to review: the Cinnabar Volcano approach includes Garrett with Slugma 16 and Magby 18 (source roster confirmed after encountering him), while the travelling party has reached level 35 after the coastal route. This may accommodate earlier access, but the level curve differs sharply from Route 12 Vera. No changes made.

The coastal party has overlapping Grass/Bug/Poison weaknesses, and the controller does not plan as well as a human player. PP depletion, missed switch opportunities and healing choices affect these outcomes. Cumulative resource use is a finding to review, not a recommendation to lower every coastal trainer.

Other usability observation: a full-party player who catches a Dream Eater learner for Sabrina at Pokémon Tower needs to visit a PC to withdraw it. Lavender’s healing house has no PC; the run returned to Saffron. No change made for this observation.

Functional fixes and their verification are recorded separately in [the playthrough log](README.md). The full campaign, remaining leaders, Apex resolutions and both Leagues are not yet certified by this run.

Koga, five-badge challenge: first attempt lost after four Super Potions. Opening Crobat 38 was defeated; Muk 40 and Arbok 39 remained. Player lead Venusaur 36, Ariados 34, with Ninetales/Budew/Chingling 30. Several party members were weak to the opposition or lacked effective attacks, and the controller repeatedly focused the first opposing slot. Venusaur also entered below full health after the volcano approach. These preparation and targeting limitations prevent treating the loss as evidence for a roster nerf. Normal blackout returned to Cinnabar; no rollback and no team changes.
