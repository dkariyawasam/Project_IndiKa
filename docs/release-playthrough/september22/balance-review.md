Additional contrast to review: the Cinnabar Volcano approach includes Garrett with Slugma 16 and Magby 18 (source roster confirmed after encountering him), while the travelling party has reached level 35 after the coastal route. This may accommodate earlier access, but the level curve differs sharply from Route 12 Vera. No changes made.

The coastal party has overlapping Grass/Bug/Poison weaknesses, and the controller does not plan as well as a human player. PP depletion, missed switch opportunities and healing choices affect these outcomes. Cumulative resource use is a finding to review, not a recommendation to lower every coastal trainer.

Other usability observation: a full-party player who catches a Dream Eater learner for Sabrina at Pokémon Tower needs to visit a PC to withdraw it. Lavender’s healing house has no PC; the run returned to Saffron. No change made for this observation.

Functional fixes and their verification are recorded separately in [the playthrough log](README.md). The full campaign, remaining leaders, Apex resolutions and both Leagues are not yet certified by this run.

Koga, five-badge challenge: first attempt lost after four Super Potions. Opening Crobat 38 was defeated; Muk 40 and Arbok 39 remained. Player lead Venusaur 36, Ariados 34, with Ninetales/Budew/Chingling 30. Several party members were weak to the opposition or lacked effective attacks, and the controller repeatedly focused the first opposing slot. Venusaur also entered below full health after the volcano approach. These preparation and targeting limitations prevent treating the loss as evidence for a roster nerf. Normal blackout returned to Cinnabar; no rollback and no team changes.

Mansion rival first attempt: opening Pidgeot 36 defeated the party before its remaining roster was seen. Venusaur entered partly depleted after Scientist Lenox; Ariados/Budew share its Flying weakness, and Ninetales/Chingling were level 30. This is primarily a preparation/coverage limitation of the test party, not enough evidence for a trainer nerf. Stronger coverage is being sought through normal captures.

### Mansion rival retry

Won after normally catching Jynx 30 in Seafoam and healing the party. Four Super Potions; no faints or Revives. Sleep/seed support plus Jynx Ice Punch and Ninetales Quick Attack succeeded against Pidgeot 36 / Raticate 34 / Kadabra 35 / Charizard 38. Venusaur reached 37 during the battle. This demonstrates a viable earned-party route, not a general balance pass: Jynx required five Repel durations to find, and earlier loss/coverage limitations remain relevant. No balance changes.

### Silph rival

First attempt won with three Super Potions and two Full Heals, no Revives. Pidgeot 37’s Wing Attack dealt 66 then 61 damage to Jynx 31 (87 max HP); sleep/seed Venusaur recovered the position. Exeggcute 38 spread paralysis; its critical Confusion knocked out Ariados. Ninetales’s Flash Fire, Confuse Ray and Venusaur’s Leech Seed handled Charizard 40. Alakazam 35 used Calm Mind/Future Sight; Gyarados 35 used Dragon Rage. Venusaur reached 40 and Ninetales 31.

Caveats: Chingling was already fainted on entry; team levels uneven. Pilot had replaced Ariados’s Pin Missile with Fury Swipes at level 35, so its intended Bug coverage was reduced. No trainer data changed.

### Silph Giovanni

First attempt won without items or faints. Venusaur entered at 40 and finished at 42, handling all six opponents with Sleep Powder, Leech Seed and Razor Leaf. Kangaskhan repeatedly chose resisted Brick Break; seed recovery exceeded that damage. Nidoqueen eventually used Sludge Bomb; Rhydon survived one super-effective Razor Leaf and used Earthquake. Gyarados remained asleep through its turns and took two critical Razor Leafs. Long favourable sleeps and critical hits substantially reduce how representative this result is. Record status/seed susceptibility and Kangaskhan move selection for review; no balance edits.

Cerulean Cave return: Venusaur exhausted Retreat PP, then Venusaur and Ariados each fainted to wild Wobbuffet Destiny Bond. The pilot attacked directly into visible Destiny Bond, so these faints are controller errors, not evidence of excessive levels. Updated the normal-input helper to stall active Destiny Bond with available support moves and prefer Leech Seed against Shadow Tag. Outcomes retained, no rollback.

### Viridian Gym at five badges

First attempt lost before defeating either opener, Sandslash 47 / Dugtrio 47. Venusaur 43 was close in level, but the rest were 30–35. Used one Full Restore; Giovanni used one Hyper Potion. Swords Dance/Slash punished the low-level support Pokémon; Earthquake hit both player slots. Three missed Sleep Powders and critical Slash worsened the opening. A late manual target-selection error aimed Sleep Powder at already-sleeping Sandslash instead of Dugtrio; do not attribute that wasted turn to game AI/balance. This demonstrates that opening Viridian early does not make its fixed late-game team suitable for this party. Aide explicitly warns it is the toughest Gym. No balance edits; retry after Koga/Blaine and preparation.

Nugget Bridge’s five-trainer challenge has a broad internal level spread: Kindra uses early-teen Pokémon, Nell uses Staryu 21/Tentacool 23/Poliwhirl 25, and Becky’s source roster is Pelipper 31/Starmie 33/Lanturn 35. Nell was defeated by Chingling 31→32 without items, ending at 39/79. Becky’s live battle remains pending. This spread deserves review independently of the fixed reward-gate defect; no team changes made.

Becky’s full 31/33/35 roster verified live. Won without items or faints using Chingling 32→33 and Venusaur 43→44, with Yawn support, a successful switch out of Starmie, and a critical Razor Leaf against Lanturn. This validates a prepared midgame party, not the suitability of that team for the early bridge challenge.

### Koga second attempt

Lost with full-health Chimecho 33 / Venusaur 44 / Budew 30 / Ninetales 31 / Jynx 31 / Ariados 35. Two Super Potions, no Revives. Defeated Arbok and Crobat; Muk and Weezing remained. Both opening Pokémon had Bite, Arbok spread paralysis and Rock Slide flinches, and Crobat punished Grass/Bug weaknesses. A critical Wing Attack knocked out Chimecho and critical Sludge knocked out Ninetales. Chimecho only has Confusion for reliable Psychic damage; Ninetales still has Ember. The low-level supporting team and weak moves are important limitations, so this is not evidence for a roster nerf. No balance changes.
