# Original Pokémon design attribution

Confirmed by the creator: **Dee Kariyawasam** designed the following Pokémon:

- Amunyte
- Kinkabuto
- Omato
- Omatops
- Kabuknight
- Kabustar
- Magikarp — Deep Form
- Feebas — Deep Form
- Aeropteryx
- Osscythe
- Mime Sr.

The in-game credit is ORIGINAL POKEMON DESIGNS / DEE KARIYAWASAM. This records character design authorship; it does not automatically assign authorship of every sprite rendition or adaptation. Other custom species are not attributed by inference.
