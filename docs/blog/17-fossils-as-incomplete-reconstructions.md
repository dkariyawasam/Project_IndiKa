# Fossils as Incomplete Reconstructions

Kinkabuto and Amunyte are the surviving original forms in this setting. They never went extinct. The familiar fossil Pokémon, and the new combinations built around them, are reconstructions made from incomplete remains.

That changes the meaning of revival. A fossil machine is interpreting limited evidence. Its output can be alive and viable without being a complete copy of the animal that left the fossil.

## Four fragments, four combinations

The Dome fragment preserves Kinkabuto's shell; the Dart fragment preserves its inner body. The Helix fragment supplies Amunyte's shell, while the Haste fragment supplies its blue body.

| Shell fragment | Body fragment | Reconstructed line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dome | Dart | Kabuto → Kabutops |
| Helix | Haste | Omanyte → Omastar |
| Helix | Dart | Omato → Omatops |
| Dome | Haste | Kabustar → Kabuknight |

Cinnabar Lab makes these combinations playable. The shared colours support the explanation visually: related shells and bodies should still look related when combined in another species. A palette decision becomes a piece of worldbuilding.

## Evolution can be an imperfect response

Kabuto's incompleteness gives Kabutops a different reading. Its evolution attempts to approximate Kinkabuto's form, but overdevelops the blades and becomes more humanoid. It is a successful adaptation by a reconstruction, rather than a perfect restoration of the ancestor.

The survivors retain Water/Bug for Kinkabuto and Water/Poison for Amunyte. The reconstructed combinations are Water/Rock: missing information has consequences beyond appearance. Their rarity also matters. If the originals were ordinary roadside encounters, the player's fragment work would lose much of its mystery.

This is the intended fiction of the mod, not a claim about official Pokémon canon or real fossil DNA.

## A wider primeval theme

Deep-form Magikarp and Feebas are imagined as ancestral dragon forms that do not need the familiar incubation-and-evolution journey to become formidable. Placing them alongside Relicanth invites comparison with another survivor. Their shared Swift Swim and their second-ability choices connect them mechanically while preserving differences.

Aeropteryx extends the theme through a Dragon/Flying relative of Aerodactyl. Its art and palette can show that relationship without assigning the same typing. The current Dragon/Flying correction is in the species data.

## How it fits the larger story

Fossil reconstruction gives the player a smaller, repeatable example of Evolution by Design. The laboratory can help life return, but it cannot erase the limits of its evidence. That sits naturally beside the larger questions of invention, intervention and control raised by Blaine, Giovanni and Mewtwo.

The [laboratory script](../../data/maps/CinnabarIsland_PokemonLab_ExperimentRoom/scripts.inc) records the implemented fragment combinations. Some custom battle backs remain unfinished; the concept and reconstruction system are further along than every visual asset.
