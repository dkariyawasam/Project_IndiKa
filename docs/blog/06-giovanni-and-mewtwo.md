# Giovanni And Mewtwo

Giovanni is the mod's throughline.

The gym leaders explore growth through trust. Apex Pokemon explore growth without trainers. Giovanni explores the opposite:

What happens when someone wants evolution as control?

## The First Scene: Pokemon Mansion

Giovanni first appears beside the shattered vat where Mewtwo escaped.

He is not giving a villain speech to the player. He is talking to the empty room:

> Empty...
> After all these years, empty.

The player catches him in a private moment. That makes the obsession clearer.

[Screenshot: Giovanni beside the broken Mewtwo vat]

## Silph Co

After Pokemon Mansion, Team Rocket occupies Saffron.

Giovanni's goal is not just money or corporate takeover. He wants the Master Ball technology because it represents perfect command:

Control made mechanical.

That makes Silph Co part of the Mewtwo story rather than a disconnected Rocket episode.

## Route 7

After Silph, Mewtwo's power tears open a path north of Route 7.

The Elite Four were sent to stop it. By the time the player arrives, the battle has already happened.

The player pieces it together through flashbacks from Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha, and Lance.

## Cerulean Cave

Giovanni reaches Mewtwo first, but cannot master it.

The player then proves what Giovanni could not understand: power is not the same as partnership.

After this scene, Viridian Gym opens.

## Viridian Gym

The final Giovanni gym battle is not about disbanding Team Rocket entirely. It is about changing direction.

He does not stop being Giovanni. He stops mistaking absolute power for strength.

That matters because he can later appear as the Rocket League champion with a new outlook.
