# Trainer Identity

FireRed uses a lot of trainer classes, but many of them share generic overworld sprites.

Pokemon Expeditions has been pushing toward a cleaner rule:

If a trainer class matters, it should look like itself.

## The Naming Pass

Several classes were renamed or cleaned up to make their relationships clearer.

Examples:

- Camper and Picknicker became Scout M and Scout F.
- Cue Ball became Roughneck.
- Battle Girl and Black Belt became Black Belt F and Black Belt M.
- Cooltrainers became Ace Trainers.
- Electrician replaced the old Rocker split.

The idea is to make class families readable.

## Basic And Advanced Classes

Some trainer classes now work as pairs:

- Youngster and Rich Boy.
- Lass and Lady.
- Hiker and Ruin Maniac.
- Biker and Roughneck.
- Bug Catcher and Bug Maniac.
- Aroma Lady and Pokemon Breeder.

This gives the world a sense of progression without needing every trainer to explain themselves.

## Palette Work

The palette pass is just as important as the sprite pass.

Some sprites were technically correct but felt out of place. Matching FireRed's colour language makes new art feel native instead of pasted in.

## Current Build Notes

Recent trainer audit work has turned several classes from placeholders into dedicated overworld identities: Rich Boy, Lass, Gentleman, Burglar, Ruin Maniac, Kindler, Hiker, Engineer, Expert M/F, Black Belt, Scientist, Pokefan, Swimmer F on land, Tuber M, Lady, Electrician, and Rocket admins such as Ariana and Petrel.

Rocker has been folded into Electrician, and Porymap visibility was fixed for the newly wired overworlds so map editing reflects the in-game result.

The roster audit also removed verified-unused trainer parties and checked that Apex Pokemon are not sitting inside ordinary trainer teams.
