# Gym Leaders As Researchers

In vanilla FireRed, gym leaders are mostly milestones. Beat them, get a badge, move on.

In Pokemon Expeditions: Kanto, they are also experts.

Oak sends the player toward them because each leader understands a different relationship between people and Pokemon.

## The Human-Guided Pillar

If Apex Pokemon show how Pokemon change without trainers, gym leaders show how Pokemon change with them.

That makes the gym challenge part of the research theme instead of a separate badge quest.

[Screenshot: Gym leader field challenge or Logbook entry]

## Field Challenges

Some gym leader encounters happen outside the usual gym structure. These are field challenges: official tests that happen where the leader's expertise makes the most sense.

The leaders do not simply hand over power. They teach through Pokemon, location, and theme.

## Themes By Leader

- Brock: ancient fascination and fossil evolution.
- Misty: change, beauty, and adaptation.
- Surge: training to achieve power.
- Erika: family and care.
- Koga: community and discipline.
- Sabrina: peace and emotional control.
- Blaine: innovation, design, and progress.
- Giovanni: trust replacing the pursuit of absolute power.

## Badge Items

Gym leaders also reward special badge-linked items and modes:

- Sabrina's finder was revised into the Marsh Finder.
- Surge's Thunder Pass unlocks mart specials.
- Koga's Soul Pass unlocks friendship checks through nurses.
- Field challenge rewards are tracked through the Logbook.

## The Logbook Angle

Gym leader trials use statuses like:

- Active
- Done
- Traded

This helps the player read the gym path as research progress, not just combat progress.
