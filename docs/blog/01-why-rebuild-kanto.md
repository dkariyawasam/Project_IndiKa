# Why Rebuild Kanto?

Pokemon Expeditions: Kanto started with a simple question:

What if Kanto was not a straight line of badges, but a fieldwork assignment?

FireRed already has the bones of a brilliant adventure. It has familiar towns, strong pacing, iconic gym leaders, and the feeling of leaving home with a Pokemon for the first time. But it also has a very fixed shape. You are mostly pushed from badge to badge, town to town, until the world opens up much later.

This mod tries to open that feeling earlier.

## The Pitch

You are not just collecting badges. You are Professor Oak's field aide, sent into Kanto to study how Pokemon grow, adapt, bond, evolve, and survive.

That changes what the journey is about.

- Gym leaders become experts with different ideas about Pokemon and people.
- Apex Pokemon show what evolution can look like without human guidance.
- Giovanni and Mewtwo become the darker mirror of the whole theme: power without trust.
- The region itself becomes less of a route and more of a place to investigate.

## Why Kanto?

Kanto is familiar enough that changes are instantly readable. If Route 2 suddenly offers more than the Viridian Forest path, players notice. If Cinnabar becomes reachable early by ferry, that feels like a real break from memory. If Giovanni appears beside a shattered vat in Pokemon Mansion, the player already knows enough to feel the threat.

The mod uses that familiarity as a design tool.

The goal is not to erase FireRed. It is to make Kanto feel like a region with hidden research threads running through it.

## The Big Design Shift

Vanilla FireRed asks:

> Can you beat the gyms and become Champion?

Pokemon Expeditions asks:

> What can Kanto teach you about how Pokemon change?

That one shift touches almost everything:

- Oak's intro was rewritten around fieldwork.
- The Pokedex and Logbook became research tools.
- The rival now points players toward alternate routes and story threads.
- Gyms, Apex Pokemon, and Giovanni form the main narrative pillars.
- Menus and UI were rebuilt to make the game feel like a field aide toolkit.

## Closing Thought

This project is not trying to make Kanto bigger just for the sake of scale. It is trying to make Kanto denser, stranger, and more worth studying.

That is the whole thesis of the mod: familiar routes, new reasons to look closer.
