# Badge-derived key item icons

- Boulder Key: grey faceted badge head and metal key shaft.
- Rainbow Key: multicoloured badge flower and gold key shaft.
- Thunder Pass: diagonally presented rectangular cream card with gold trim and a central golden sun badge.
- Soul Pass: diagonally presented rectangular cream card with purple trim and a central pink-purple heart badge.
- Marsh Finder: golden circular badge housing a detector screen and antenna.
